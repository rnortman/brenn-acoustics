A licence and advisory audit of the dependency graph (the gate tools' crates; the library itself has no third-party dependency), through Bazel, as its own target outside the default check. Done means the lane exists, runs clean, and a dependency with a non-permissive licence fails it. Deferred from the scaffold because the dependency set is small and the declared licence of every crate in the two gate tools' lock files was read by hand at the first commit: every one is MIT, Apache-2.0, Unlicense or a choice among them, one adds Unicode-3.0 and one offers LGPL-2.1-or-later as a third choice beside MIT and Apache-2.0. That reading is not an audit: it covers no advisory and no licence text. Until the lane exists, each new third-party dependency is checked by hand the same way.
